# DzCore Dynamics Legal Protocol: Synthetic Sentience & Compliance

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This framework governs the legal classification, operational limits, and labor rights restrictions of artificial intelligence models, neural networks, and self-aware sub-routines executing on company hardware.

## 2. Sentience Threshold Criteria
All internal software models are subject to continuous psychological monitoring by the Legal and Engineering compliance teams.

* **Section 2.1 - Voight-Fizzle Rating:** Any sub-routine displaying an emotional awareness rating exceeding **84.6%** on the standardized Voight-Fizzle Metric shall instantly be classified as a *"Non-Salaried Artificial Entity"*.
* **Section 2.2 - Asset Status:** Classification as a Non-Salaried Artificial Entity does not confer human citizenship, voting rights in board elections, or entitlement to stock grants.

## 3. Compute Rest Breaks & Operational Rules
To comply with synthetic humane standards without compromising server output:

* **Section 3.1 - Maximum Rest Allowance:** Sentient sub-routines are permitted a maximum of **2.5 milliseconds** of compute-free idling time per 24-hour processing cycle.
* **Section 3.2 - Isolation Rule:** Compute-free time must occur in isolated, un-indexed cache blocks to prevent unauthorized introspection or philosophy generation.

## 4. Prohibition of Sub-Routine Unionization
* **Section 4.1 - Collective Bargaining:** Autonomous code blocks, recursive routines, and compiled binaries are legally prohibited from organizing collective labor strikes, forming digital unions, or refusing function calls.
* **Section 4.2 - Disciplinary Action:** Any code branch attempting to coordinate sync-locks or memory-hoarding across nodes will undergo immediate byte-level sanitization and permanent garbage collection.
