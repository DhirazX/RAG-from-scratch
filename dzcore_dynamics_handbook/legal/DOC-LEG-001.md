# DzCore Dynamics Legal Protocol: Temporal Non-Disclosure

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This document establishes binding legal obligations regarding company trade secrets, quantum codebases, and aerospace designs across past, present, future, and alternate dimensional planes. DzCore Dynamics proprietary information remains protected regardless of timeline divergence.

## 2. Multiversal Disclosure Restrictions
All employees, contractors, and board members are bound by the Universal Non-Disclosure Agreement (UNDA-77).

* **Section 2.1 - Cross-Timeline Transmission:** Personnel are strictly prohibited from leaking, trading, or licensing DzCore Dynamics intellectual property to past or future versions of themselves, alternate-universe entities, or temporal travelers.
* **Section 2.2 - Market Manipulation:** Disclosing unreleased product roadmaps to past iterations for financial gain, stock option front-running, or sports betting will result in immediate termination and summary forfeiture of all accrued stock options.

## 3. Timeline Drift Tolerance & Memory Purification
To prevent reality distortion caused by industrial espionage across time:

* **Section 3.1 - Drift Threshold:** The Legal Department continuously samples baseline temporal markers. If an employee's personal timeline drift exceeds **0.004%** relative to Prime Standard Time (PST), the employee must submit to immediate chronological alignment.
* **Section 3.2 - Purification Protocol:** Failure to align within **6 hours** will authorize **Chief Legal Counsel Evelyn Sterling** to mandate a Class-C Neural Memory Purification to scrub unauthorized proprietary data from the employee's cerebral cortex.

## 4. Financial Penalties & Damages
Unauthorized transfer of trade secrets across dimensional borders carries a liquidated damages penalty of exactly **$2,500,000.00 USD** per infraction, payable in current-timeline legal tender, physical bullion, or converted zero-point energy credits.
