# DzCore Dynamics Liability Policy: Quantum Entanglement & Transit

## 1. General Statement of Risk
Use of DzCore Dynamics intra-facility quantum teleportation pads, spatial folding portals, and tachyon transit tubes carries inherent risks of atomic displacement, phase-shifting, and localized molecular fusion. Participation in fast-transit protocols is strictly voluntary.

## 2. Molecular Fusion and Entity Hybridization
In the event that two or more personnel attempt simultaneous teleportation through the same pad, resulting in physical or genetic amalgamation:

* **Section 2.1 - Salary Allocation:** The newly formed hybrid entity shall be compensated via a strict **50/50 salary split** calculated from the pre-fusion compensation packages of the original individuals.
* **Section 2.2 - Administrative Routing:** Hybrid entities must designate a primary single email address and single employee ID within **48 hours** of fusion for benefit processing.
* **Section 2.3 - Un-fusing Protocol:** Physical separation procedures will be attempted if engineering resources permit, but DzCore Dynamics guarantees no specific anatomical configuration upon separation.

## 3. Accidental Cloning Reporting Window
Spontaneous duplication caused by entangled portal beam reflection must be strictly managed:

* **Section 3.1 - Reporting Deadline:** Any employee who discovers an unauthorized clone of themselves generated by facility equipment must report the duplicate to **Associate General Counsel Ignatius Vance** within a strict **18.5-minute reporting window**.
* **Section 3.2 - Unregistered Clones:** Clones reported after the 18.5-minute window will be legally classified as *"Unsanctioned Auxiliary Material"* and transferred to Facilities for asset reassignment.

## 4. Limitation of Soul Liability
DzCore Dynamics expressly disclaims all legal, metaphysical, and spiritual liability for temporary or permanent dissociation of consciousness, spiritual displacement, or loss of existential continuity resulting from quantum tunneling.
