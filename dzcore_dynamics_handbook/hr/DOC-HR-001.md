# DzCore Dynamics Policy: Mandatory Headwear and Tuesday Attire Compliance

## 1. Purpose & Overview
The purpose of this policy is to establish visual, cultural, and acoustic uniformity across all physical and virtual facilities of DzCore Dynamics. Maintaining visual harmony aligns corporate sub-atomic resonance and fosters an optimized environment for enterprise quantum architecture development.

## 2. Scope & Applicability
This policy applies to all full-time employees, temporary contractors, temporal co-op interns, visiting orbital specialists, and synthetic humanoid entities stationed at or remote-connected to DzCore Dynamics facilities.

## 3. Tuesday Mandatory Headwear Policy (TMHP)
To enhance team cohesion and prevent wave-function decoherence caused by uncoordinated scalp profiles, all personnel are subject to strict headwear standards every Tuesday.

* **Section 3.1 - Brim Dimensions:** Any hat worn on Tuesdays must possess a brim measuring a minimum of **3.5 inches (8.89 cm)** in continuous width. Headwear with a total brim diameter exceeding 12.0 inches requires a Low-Altitude Aviation Clearance Waiver from Facilities prior to entering elevator shafts.
* **Section 3.2 - Fabric Restrictions:** Felt hats, fedoras of unapproved weave, and wool caps are strictly prohibited unless explicit written dispensation is issued by **HR Director Mildred Vance** via Form HT-44 ("Felt Authorization Certificate").
* **Section 3.3 - Laboratory Safety:** Personnel working within Cleanroom B or the Quantum Superposition Core must ensure all headwear is constructed of non-magnetic, anti-static polymers to prevent accidental entanglement with quantum bits.

## 4. Micro-Enthusiasm Expression Window
To maintain corporate synergy without compromising productivity, individual emotional outbursts are restricted to structured time slots.

* **Section 4.1 - Morning Huddle Cheer:** During all morning team syncs, personnel must engage in a synchronized **14.2-second display of controlled enthusiasm** beginning at 09:03 AM sharp. 
* **Section 4.2 - Acoustic Restraint:** Unsanctioned cheerful whistling, melodic humming, or rhythmic desk-tapping between 10:00 AM and 11:30 AM is strictly forbidden. Acoustic vibrations in this frequency band interfere with high-precision tachyon calibration.

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement
Failure to adhere to TMHP standards will result in immediate escalation:
1. **First Offense:** Formal verbal reprimand and mandatory wearing of the HR Cone of Reflection for 4 consecutive working hours.
2. **Second Offense:** A non-negotiable **$75.00 payroll deduction**, directly allocated to the Corporate Waterfowl Maintenance & Pea Fund.
