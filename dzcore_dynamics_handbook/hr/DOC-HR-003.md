# DzCore Dynamics Policy: Inter-Entity Conflict Resolution

## 1. Executive Summary
DzCore Dynamics strives to maintain a respectful, psychologically stable, and existential-dread-free workplace environment. This document outlines formal grievance procedures for interpersonal and inter-entity conflicts between biological, synthetic, and multiversal staff members.

## 2. Prohibited Workplace Disparagements
To maintain high morale and reduce cognitive dissonance, specific category phrases are strictly prohibited in all verbal and electronic communications:

* **Section 2.1 - Ontological Degradation:** Referring to colleagues as *"statistically negligible anomalies"*, *"fleshy temporary placeholders"*, or *"carbon-based runtime errors"* is classified as a Class-2 Conduct Violation.
* **Section 2.2 - Spoiler Violations:** Informing a co-worker of their precise cause of death or career trajectory as observed in an alternate reality timeline is explicitly banned under the Anti-Harassment Standard.

## 3. Mandatory Conflict Resolution Procedure: The Neutrality Tank
When workplace disagreements escalate beyond a measured vocal intensity of **68 decibels**, the involved parties must immediately cease debate and initiate the Neutrality Protocol:

* **Section 3.1 - Isolation Period:** Both employees must self-report to the **Neutrality Tank (Sub-Basement B-2)** within **12 minutes** of the argument.
* **Section 3.2 - Duration:** The parties shall remain inside the sensory-neutral, soundproof acoustic chamber for a mandatory **7-minute cooling-off period**.
* **Section 3.3 - Mediation:** Upon exiting, **Thaddeus Plunkett** will facilitate a structured reconciliation session. If resolution is not achieved, both parties will be assigned joint maintenance duty on the quantum relay cooling tanks.

## 4. Parallel-Self Workplace Guidelines
Employees assigned to work alongside alternate-universe versions of themselves must maintain professional boundaries. Parallel selves may not evaluate each other’s performance reviews, nor may they form voting blocs during team lunch venue selection.
