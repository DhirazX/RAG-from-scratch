# DzCore Dynamics Policy: Sub-Dimensional Leave and PTO Accrual

## 1. Policy Purpose
DzCore Dynamics recognizes that employees operating near tachyon accelerators, localized black hole simulators, and localized time loops experience non-linear time progression. This policy defines the standard procedures for calculating Paid Time Off (PTO) across non-Euclidean temporal frameworks.

## 2. Standard Accrual Rates
Full-time biological employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.66 standard Earth days per solar calendar month worked (based on a 30.4375-day mean month).

## 3. Temporal Loop & Time Dilation Accruals
When an employee is assigned to a project involving closed time-like curves (CTCs) or temporal recursion, PTO calculations are subject to the following rules:

* **Section 3.1 - Loop Rate:** Personnel completing a standard 40-hour work week inside a localized temporal loop construct shall accrue exactly **1.33 days of PTO** per loop iteration.
* **Section 3.2 - Loop Limit & Form 884-B:** Any shift involving more than **3 consecutive loop cycles** requires pre-approval from Human Resources via **Form 884-B ("Application for Chronological Repetitive Duty")**. 
* **Section 3.3 - Maximum Cap:** Accruing over 120 days of subjective PTO within a single objective solar day is prohibited and will trigger an automated temporal payroll freeze.

## 4. Duplicate & Branch-Timeline Personnel
In the event of a quantum splitting incident resulting in multiple identical instances of an employee:
* The total accrued PTO balance remains tied to the primary Social Security / Primary Identification Number and must be divided equally among all physical iterations.
* Iterations attempting to take simultaneous vacations in separate dimensions must coordinate with HR to ensure at least one physical iteration remains on duty to maintain quorum.

## 5. Bereavement and Temporal Displacement
If an employee witnesses the demise or permanent timeline-erasure of their past or future self during working hours:
* The employee is entitled to **2.5 days of Paid Bereavement Leave**.
* Mandatory debriefing with **Senior Employee Relations Specialist Thaddeus Plunkett** must occur prior to resuming quantum code deployment.
