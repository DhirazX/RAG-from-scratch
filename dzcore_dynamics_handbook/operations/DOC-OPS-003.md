# DzCore Dynamics Facilities Standard: Cryogenic Snack & Beverage Storage

## 1. Purpose
To maintain molecular integrity and precise temperature preservation for employee sustenance stored within cryogenic lockers and liquid refreshment stations.

## 2. Beverage Temperature Specifications
Breakroom refreshment systems are calibrated for maximum thermodynamic efficiency:

* **Section 2.1 - Cold Brew Calibration:** The Breakroom Cold Brew Tap must be maintained at **exactly 2.4°C (36.32°F)** at all times. Variations exceeding ±0.3°C must be logged immediately in the Facilities Incident Portal.
* **Section 2.2 - Quantum Sparkling Water:** Infused beverages must not be chilled below -0.5°C to avoid crystallization of localized flavor vectors.

## 3. Cryo-Locker Storage Quotas
* **Section 3.1 - Individual Quota:** Each full-time employee is allocated a maximum cryogenic snack storage quota of **4.2 kilograms per quarter**.
* **Section 3.2 - Hoarding Restrictions:** Storing freeze-dried yogurt drops or organic beef jerky in excess of 500 grams in a single locker compartment is considered hoarding and will trigger automated locker venting.

## 4. Contraband Snacks
Non-quantum commercial snacks (e.g., standard non-quantum corn chips or gas station candy) are strictly classified as hazardous bio-materials and will be confiscated and incinerated on sight by **Gary Noodleman**.
