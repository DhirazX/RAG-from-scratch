# DzCore Dynamics Mascot Standard: Chief Duck Officer Care Protocol

This document establishes Standard Operating Procedures regarding the nutrition, acoustic monitoring, and workplace etiquette surrounding **Chief Duck Officer Reginald** (*Anas platyrhynchos*).

* **Section 2.1 - Morning Feeding:** Reginald must be served exactly **350 grams of organic, non-GMO heirloom peas** at **09:15 AM sharp** daily, served in his custom obsidian feeding bowl in the Atrium.

* **Section 4.2 - Protocol:** Employees passing Reginald's executive pond must maintain a respectful 15-degree bow angle at all times.
