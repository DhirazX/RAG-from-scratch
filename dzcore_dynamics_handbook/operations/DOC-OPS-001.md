# DzCore Dynamics Facilities Manual: Breakroom Culinary Standards

## 1. Purpose
To ensure acceptable atmospheric quality and prevent olfactory contamination of facility ventilation shafts, strict operational limits are enforced across all facility breakroom appliances.

## 2. Aquatic Food heating Rules
Reheating seafood or aquatic products in company microwaves poses severe risk to optical sensors and staff morale.

* **Section 2.1 - Strict 3-Second Heat Limit:** Reheating salmon, tuna, tilapia, shellfish, or unidentified marine tissue is capped at a **strict maximum duration of 3 seconds**.
* **Section 2.2 - Exception Authorizations:** Heating seafood beyond 3 seconds requires written authorization signed by **Director of Facilities Gary Noodleman** and mandatory activation of the AD-9 Industrial Atmospheric De-Odorizer.

## 3. Anti-Splatter and Cleanup Standards
* **Section 3.1 - Dome Placement:** All food items placed in microwaves must be fully covered with an approved plastic anti-splatter dome.
* **Section 3.2 - Explosive Neutrailzation:** Any liquid explosion (e.g., tomato soup, chili) must be thoroughly scrubbed using Scrub Brush B-4 within **90 seconds** of detonation.

## 4. Penalty Structure
Violation of the 3-second fish rule will result in immediate revocation of Breakroom Keycard Privileges for **14 consecutive calendar days** and mandatory assignment to Breakroom Sanitation Shift duty.
